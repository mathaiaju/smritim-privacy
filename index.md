# SMRITI-M Privacy Policy

*Version 1.0*

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

## 1. About SMRITI-M

SMRITI-M is a digital medication-monitoring and care-linkage application
developed by the Department of Pharmacology and Department of
Psychiatry, JMMC & RI.

SMRITI-M is intended to support medication management and adherence
monitoring, patient-entered mood/symptom and sleep monitoring, adverse
drug reaction (ADR) reporting, and communication among patients,
authorized caregivers, and treating healthcare professionals.

SMRITI-M provides advisory monitoring and alerts to support clinical
review. It does not independently diagnose a medical condition,
prescribe medication, change medication, or make autonomous treatment
decisions. Clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating
healthcare professional.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the user's role and use of the application, SMRITI-M may
collect the following information:

**Personal information:** name, age, gender, phone number, and email
address.

**Account and authentication information:** information required to
create, authenticate, secure, and maintain a user account, including
login-related information.

**Medication and monitoring information:** medication list,
medication-taking/adherence information, patient-entered mood and
symptom information, sleep information, and adverse drug
reaction/side-effect information.

The current application does not collect a psychiatric diagnosis as an
app data field. It does not collect photographs or unrelated health
information outside the application's defined functions.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information collected through SMRITI-M to create and maintain
accounts; provide medication and adherence monitoring; display the
user's medication information; receive patient-entered mood, symptom,
and sleep information; identify predefined monitoring or safety signals;
support communication with treating clinicians and authorized
caregivers; facilitate ADR reporting; support approved research
activities relating to SMRITI-M; maintain application functionality and
security; provide technical support; and comply with applicable
institutional, legal, and regulatory requirements.

## 4. Clinical Decision Support

SMRITI-M is clinician-governed and advisory. Alerts, monitoring
information, and risk-related signals are intended to support clinical
review.

SMRITI-M does not independently diagnose patients, prescribe medicines,
start or stop medicines, change medication doses, or make autonomous
treatment decisions. The treating healthcare professional retains
responsibility for clinical assessment and treatment decisions.

## 5. Who May Access Information

Access is role-based and limited to information needed for the relevant
function.

Patients may access information relating to their own account and
monitoring.

Authorized caregivers may receive relevant monitoring information where
caregiver involvement is enabled and authorized.

Treating psychiatrists/clinicians may access relevant medication and
monitoring information required for clinical review.

Authorized members of the research team may access information required
for the approved SMRITI-M research activities, subject to applicable
ethical and institutional requirements.

Authorized technical administrators/developers may access information
where necessary for system administration, security, troubleshooting,
maintenance, or technical support.

The pharmacovigilance/ADR team receives ADR-related and medication
information through the institutional ADR reporting pathway. The ADR
communication does not include the patient's name, phone number, or
email address; a hospital ID may be included where necessary for
clarification through appropriate institutional processes.

## 6. Data Sharing

SMRITI-M does not sell personal or health information.

Information may be disclosed to authorized parties where necessary for
the functions described in this policy, including treating clinicians,
authorized caregivers, authorized research personnel, technical
administrators, and the institutional pharmacovigilance/ADR team through
the limited ADR pathway described above.

The SMRITI-M application uses an OpenAI large language model for
generic/non-patient prompts. Patient-identifiable information and
patient health information are not sent to OpenAI through SMRITI-M.

## 7. Data Storage and Infrastructure

SMRITI-M uses a cloud-hosted backend on Google Cloud. The application
communicates with the backend through an HTTPS API. The backend database
is MySQL installed on a Google Cloud Compute Engine virtual machine.

The application does not use Firebase, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM),
or Crashlytics.

Access to stored information is restricted according to user roles and
operational requirements.

## 8. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures intended to
protect personal and health information against unauthorized access,
alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

These measures include HTTPS for data transmission between the
application and backend, authentication and access controls, role-based
access where implemented, restricted administrative access, secure
server administration, and appropriate backup and recovery measures.

The specific configuration of encryption for data at rest may depend on
the Google Cloud VM and storage configuration. This policy will be
updated if the technical configuration or security controls materially
change.

## 9. Data Retention

Personal and health information is retained for as long as reasonably
necessary to provide SMRITI-M functionality, maintain accounts and
security, support approved research activities, support
medication-safety/ADR processes, meet institutional requirements, or
comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

When information is no longer required for a legitimate purpose and
there is no applicable retention requirement, it will be deleted or
appropriately de-identified.

Where certain information must be retained after an account-deletion
request for legitimate legal, regulatory, institutional,
research-integrity, security, or medication-safety reasons, the retained
information will be limited to what is necessary and handled under
applicable safeguards.

## 10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SMRITI-M account and associated
personal data. SMRITI-M provides an in-app account-deletion pathway and
will provide an external web-based mechanism for users who cannot access
the application.

Account-deletion requests may also be initiated by contacting
**smritimplaystore@gmail.com**. The request may require reasonable
verification of the requester's identity or account ownership.

When an account is deleted, associated user data will be deleted unless
specific information must be retained for legitimate legal, regulatory,
institutional, security, research-integrity, or medication-safety
reasons. Any such retention will be limited to what is required and will
be described to the user where appropriate.

## 11. Research Use

SMRITI-M is initially being used in connection with research evaluating
a risk-stratified digital monitoring and care-linkage framework.
Research activities may evaluate medication adherence, continuity of
care, implementation outcomes, usability, feasibility, and
medication-safety reporting.

Research information will be handled in accordance with the approved
research protocol, applicable ethics requirements, institutional
policies, and confidentiality safeguards. Where appropriate, research
datasets will be coded or de-identified before analysis.

## 12. Caregiver Access

Where a patient has authorized caregiver involvement, relevant
monitoring information may be made available to the caregiver according
to the permissions and functionality configured in SMRITI-M.

Caregiver access is intended to support medication adherence,
recognition of relevant changes, communication with the care team, and
timely care-seeking. Caregiver access does not provide authority to
independently modify treatment through SMRITI-M.

## 13. Emergency Use

SMRITI-M is not an emergency medical service and should not be relied
upon as a substitute for emergency medical care. In an emergency or when
immediate medical attention is required, users should contact the
appropriate emergency medical service or healthcare facility.

## 14. Children

SMRITI-M is intended for adult users in the relevant clinical/research
setting. The current research protocol specifies participants aged 18
years or older. The application is not intended to knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## 15. Third-Party Services

SMRITI-M uses Google Cloud infrastructure for its backend environment.
The application does not use Firebase, Firebase Cloud Messaging, or
Crashlytics.

SMRITI-M uses OpenAI only for generic/non-patient prompts. No
patient-identifiable information or patient health information is sent
to OpenAI through the application.

Third-party infrastructure or service providers may be updated as the
application evolves. Any material change affecting personal or health
information handling will be reflected in an updated privacy policy
where required.

## 16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SMRITI-M's functionality, data
practices, infrastructure, or applicable requirements change. The
effective date and last-updated date will be revised when material
changes are made.
