# SMRITI-M Account and Data Deletion

## Request deletion

SMRITI-M users may request deletion of their account and associated
personal data.

To request deletion, email:

**smritimplaystore@gmail.com**

Please include the email address or other account identifier associated
with your SMRITI-M account. We may request reasonable verification of
account ownership before processing the request.

When an account is deleted, associated user data will be deleted unless
specific information must be retained for legitimate legal, regulatory,
institutional, security, research-integrity, or medication-safety
reasons. Any retained information will be limited to what is required
and handled under applicable safeguards.

For questions about privacy or data deletion, contact
**smritimplaystore@gmail.com**.
